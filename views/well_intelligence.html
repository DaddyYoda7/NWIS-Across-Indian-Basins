Discovery Genesis & Pre-Drilling Geoscience

Proven Breakthrough

How The Deposit Was Found:

Drilled as a bold wildcat test into the alluvial plain of the Burhi Dihing river valley near Duliajan based purely on reflection seismic surveys. At the time, industry consensus believed oil in Assam was confined solely to exposed foothill anticlines (like Digboi). NHK-1 shattered this paradigm by proving prolific oil reservoirs lay trapped deep beneath the thick Brahmaputra river alluvium.

Pre-Drilling Geosciences & Surveys:

2D reflection seismic surveys executed between 1937 and 1951 through riverine silt deposits. Identified deep faulted rollover anticlinal structure at 10,000+ ft depth. Gravity and magnetic anomalies confirmed regional basement deepening towards the Himalayan foredeep.

Exploration Paradigm Shift:

Proved commercial hydrocarbons in the Barail Group beneath alluvium. Led to the discovery of Moran field (1956), the incorporation of Oil India Limited (OIL) in 1959, and construction of India’s first modern long-distance crude pipeline (Nahorkatiya-Guwahati-Barauni, 1,157 km).

Drilling Engineering & Mechanics

National-130 Heavy Rotary
Drilling Mechanism:

Heavy steam/diesel rotary rig with 142-ft derrick. First deep rotary test exceeding 10,000 ft in Southeast Asia.

Mud Circulation System:

High-density freshwater bentonite-caustic-quebracho mud, weighted with barite to 1.36-1.42 SG to balance high tectonic pore pressures.

Bit & Hole Assembly Program:

26-inch hole to 350 m (Tricone roller bits); 17-1/2-inch to 1,200 m; 12-1/4-inch to 2,850 m; 8-1/2-inch reservoir drilling to 3,571 m TD.

Drilling Conditions & Difficulties Encountered

Operational Hurdles
Tectonic Stress & Compressive Faulting:

Extreme horizontal tectonic compression from the Himalayan collision front and Naga thrust caused borehole deformation and high differential horizontal stress.

Shale Sloughing & Swelling Clays:

Severe hydration and mechanical spalling of brittle overpressured Barail shales and Girujan clays caused recurrent drillstring drag and pipe sticking during trips.

Circulation Losses & Gas Influxes:

Encountered 420 bbl fluid loss at 2,862 m MD into natural fractures in Tipam transition, cured with 60 bbl fibrous LCM pill. Pore pressure gas kick at 3,120 m killed with 1.38 SG mud.

Surface & Environmental Logistics:

Torrential Brahmaputra monsoon floods, malaria epidemics in jungle camps, soft marshland requiring thousands of sal-wood logs for corduroy access roads.
