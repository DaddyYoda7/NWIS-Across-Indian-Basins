psychology How It Was Found & Pre-Drilling Data:

Drilled as a bold wildcat test into the alluvial plain based purely on reflection seismic surveys. Proved for the first time that oil lay trapped deep beneath river alluvium outside the exposed hill anticlines.

warning Drilling Difficulties Encountered:

Encountered 420 bbl fluid loss at 2,862m MD in lower Tipam transition. Overpressured Barail shales required 1.38 SG mud to prevent sloughing and drillstring drag.
